Privacy Policy

ADHD Life Coach (including Aviva and Aviva Pro). Last updated: November 17, 2026.

This app is built to work without an account. Most of what you enter stays on your device. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, when, and why.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, routines, schedule, school courses and assignments, energy and stress check-ins, cycle tracking, workouts, body measurements, meals, reflections and settings are saved in your device's local storage. We do not receive or store a copy of them on our servers, unless you turn on cloud backup (see below).

What leaves your device, and why

  • AI features (Aviva). When you use an AI feature (for example a brain dump, a task breakdown, the coach, the syllabus scanner, or an AI meal plan, recipe or workout program), the text or image you submit, plus limited context such as your energy level, coaching style and relevant preferences, is sent through our server to our AI provider, Groq, to generate a response. We do not store these requests on our server.
  • Food lookups. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search term or barcode is sent to Open Food Facts or the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) FoodData Central to look up nutrition information.
  • Optional cloud backup. If you choose to back up with your email address, your email is used to sign you in, and your setup profile (your onboarding answers and preferences) is stored with our database provider, Supabase, so you can restore it on a new device. You can delete this account and its data at any time in the app: You > Delete my cloud backup account.
  • Purchases (Aviva Pro). Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To confirm that you have Pro, we use a random purchase identifier that is not linked to your name or email, and we check it with Apple and, if enabled, with our subscription service provider, RevenueCat.
  • Hosting. Our server is hosted by Vercel. Like most web services, it receives your IP address with each request. We use it only briefly, to prevent abuse (rate limiting), and don't store it ourselves. Vercel may keep standard server logs for a limited time.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • Your health-related entries (such as cycle tracking, medication reminders, injuries and body measurements) are not sent to us. They may only leave your device if you include them in text you send to an AI feature.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan a food barcode or photograph a syllabus. Photos are used only for the feature you chose. A syllabus photo is sent to the AI provider to read the assignments, and nothing else is uploaded.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Use the app fully without an account or cloud backup.
  • Export your data any time: You > Export my data.
  • Delete your cloud backup account any time in the app.
  • Delete all on-device data by deleting the app (or clearing this site's data in your browser).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
